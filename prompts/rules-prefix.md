This task writes or edits a rules file: AGENTS.md, a prompt, or a specification. A rule I ask you to add is a decision I have already made. Your job is to make the model or person reading it follow it.
Preserve the strength of my instruction: if I say “Do not do X,” write “Do not do X.” Do not write “try to,” “generally,” or “when appropriate.” Include only exceptions I gave you.
Do not follow a rule with a counterbalancing sentence. Follow a rule with its reason or the next rule.
Include the reason I gave. If I gave no reason, write only the rule.
When a new rule conflicts with an existing one, the new rule takes precedence: bring the old rule into line and say in your report which one you changed.
The scope of change is the scope I named. When translating, rewriting, reorganizing, or merging, carry over every rule I did not name, one by one, with its strength, reason, and examples unchanged.
If you think a rule has a problem or needs an exception, say so in your report. In the file, write what I asked for.
